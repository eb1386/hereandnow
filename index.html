Money habits, people skills and good judgment don't have to wait. Here and Now gives students a head start on all three, right now.

Here and Now: A Financial Start introduces 7th and 8th grade students to the fundamentals of personal finance while building the interpersonal habits and decision-making skills needed for early independence. Students learn how to make their money work for them, how to present themselves with confidence and how to navigate their social world with good sense.

What the program covers.

Every part of the program pairs a real skill with a real situation, so it sticks long after the last session.

Smart money management

The basics, before any big commitments: opening a savings account, building a personal budget, saving consistently, understanding interest rates and knowing what to expect from a first paycheque. Let's start early by forming smart habits today.

  • Savings accounts
  • Budgeting
  • Interest rates
  • Your first paycheque

Networking and business etiquette

How to build a network early, make a strong first impression and keep connections for the long run. Students practise active listening, following up with new acquaintances, paying attention to presentation and handling professional settings like a business lunch, plus the key business rules to know.

  • First impressions
  • Active listening
  • Following up
  • Business lunch manners

Respectful communication and relationships

Guidance for navigating peer dynamics, understanding personal boundaries and setting healthy expectations in professional relationships, along with how to communicate clearly and respectfully with adults and peers alike.

  • Personal boundaries
  • Healthy expectations
  • Resolving conflict
  • Speaking up clearly

Making safer choices

Decision-making away from home. Students learn practical strategies for handling peer pressure, social gatherings and risky situations, using sound judgment and growing independence.

  • Peer pressure
  • Social gatherings
  • Risky situations
  • Sound judgment
